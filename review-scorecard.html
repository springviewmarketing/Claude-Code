    Star rating

    Northwestern's Spiegel Research Center found purchase likelihood peaks between 4.0 and 4.7 stars and falls as ratings approach 5.0. In no category was a perfect score optimal. A flawless 5.0 on low volume reads as bought.

    Matched to your patient flow. Localo's study of 335,520 deleted reviews found profiles gaining one or two a week kept them for 594 days, while profiles gaining 100 or more lost them within six.
